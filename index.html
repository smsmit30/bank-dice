BANK!

A dice game of nerve. Build the pot together, then bank before a 7 wipes it out.

Dice

How it works
  • Each round, everyone shares one pot. Roll two dice and add the total.
  • First 3 rolls: a 7 is worth 70 points, and doubles count as their face total.
  • After 3 rolls: a 7 ends the round and the pot is lost. Doubles multiply the pot.
  • Tap Bank to take the pot and sit out the rest of the round. Most points after the last round wins.
